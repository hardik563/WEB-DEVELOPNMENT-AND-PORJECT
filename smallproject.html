BIOGRAPHY

Shin-chan (Shinnosuke Nohara) is a mischievous, five-year-old Japanese kindergartener created by mangaka Yoshito Usui in 1990. Known for his brutally honest, humorous antics, Shin-chan lives in Kasukabe, Japan, with his family and is famous for his love of chocolates and the superhero Action Mask

ACHIEVEMENTS

  • 1997

    Shin-chan has been adapted into an anime series, which has gained international popularity.

  • 2000

    The character has appeared in numerous films and video games.

  • 2005

    Shin-chan's unique humor and personality have made him a beloved figure in Japanese pop culture.
