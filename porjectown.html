About Dr. A.P.J. Abdul Kalam

Watch a short video about Dr. A.P.J. Abdul Kalam

Dr. Avul Pakir Jainulabdeen Abdul Kalam was born on 15 October 1931 in Rameswaram, Tamil Nadu. He was one of India's greatest scientists and served as the 11th President of India from 2002 to 2007. He was popularly known as the "Missile Man of India" because of his important contributions to India's missile and space programs.

Education

  • Schooling in Rameswaram
  • St. Joseph's College, Tiruchirappalli
  • Madras Institute of Technology (MIT)

Major Achievements

  • Developed India's missile technology.
  • Played a key role in Pokhran-II Nuclear Tests (1998).
  • Served as the 11th President of India.
  • Inspired millions of students through his speeches.
  • Authored many famous books.

Awards

  • Padma Bhushan (1981)
  • Padma Vibhushan (1990)
  • Bharat Ratna (1997)

Famous Books

  1. Wings of Fire
  2. Ignited Minds
  3. India 2020
  4. My Journey

Famous Quote

"Dream, dream, dream. Dreams transform into thoughts and thoughts result in action."

Conclusion

Dr. A.P.J. Abdul Kalam dedicated his life to science, education, and the development of India. His simplicity, honesty, and vision continue to inspire millions of people around the world. He will always be remembered as the "People's President."
